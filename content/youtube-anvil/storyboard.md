# Storyboard: ANVIL, an open source software factory

Frame: 1920x1080, 30 fps. Every visual is a committed file under content/youtube-anvil/assets/ (or, for the close, a card rendered from text). Visuals may be cropped and scaled, then padded to 1920x1080 with the paper background of the committed assets. On screen text is burned in as a title at the start of its section, exactly as written below. Palette: paper background, ink green, teal and coral, as in the committed diagrams.

## 1. Hook

- Visual: assets/sentia-page-hero.png
- On screen text: none
- Framing note: crop to the top band of the frame (the headline and the photo of the patch on the arm, down to just above the body paragraph) and scale to full width, padded top and bottom with paper. The crop keeps the body paragraph, the spec row and the metric tiles out of view, because they carry product details and third party network names that are not on the claim list. Hold still; no text is added.

## 2. The line

- Visual: assets/diagram-line.png
- On screen text: "One intake. One line."
- Framing note: show the whole diagram, scaled to fit and centered (INTAKE, SPEC, BUILD, REVIEW, HUMAN GATE). Place the title in ink green in the empty paper area above the row of boxes so it never overlaps a label.

## 3. Verified memory

- Visual: assets/diagram-memory.png
- On screen text: "Verified memory."
- Framing note: show the whole diagram, scaled to fit and centered. Place the title in the open paper area at the top, clear of both halves, so it reads as a heading for the split between VERIFIED MEMORY and QUARANTINE without covering either label.

## 4. Proof

- Visual: assets/sentia-dashboard.png
- On screen text: "Designed and built end to end by ANVIL."
- Framing note: crop out the top of the frame, including the sentence fragment and the row of tab names, so no personal name appears on screen. Keep the left two columns of metric tiles and the charts below them; crop out the right column, which names a third party network and phone. Scale up to fill the frame, pad with paper if the aspect ratio needs it, and place the title in a band at the top in ink green.

## 5. Close

- Visual: a title card rendered from text over a dark ink background (no asset file)
- On screen text: "ANVIL" and "An open source software factory." and "github.com/arananet/anvil-factory"
- Framing note: three centered lines on dark ink. "ANVIL" largest, in paper white; "An open source software factory." below it at a medium size; "github.com/arananet/anvil-factory" smallest, in teal, at the bottom of the group. Hold the card for the whole section.
