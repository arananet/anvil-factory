---
name: media-production
description: Produce images, audio and video that meet a stated quality bar, stay consistent across a set, and carry a record of how each asset was made. Use when generating or assembling media for a piece or a product.
---

Never forward an asset you have not looked at or listened to. Generation is a draft; selection and correction are the work.

**Plan before generating**
- From the brief and the draft, list each asset: what it must show, where it is used, its size, aspect ratio and duration, and what the viewer should notice first.
- Fix the look for the whole set before the first asset: palette, light direction and quality, lens and framing, line and texture, type treatment. Write it down as a short style sheet and reuse it word for word.
- Use the project's reference anchors and design system as the bar. If there are none, say so and state the standard you are working to.

**Direct each asset specifically**
- Describe subject, action, setting, composition, light, material and mood in concrete terms. "A matte aluminium device on dark slate, lit from upper left by one soft key, shallow depth of field" directs; "a cool product shot" does not.
- Compose on purpose: one subject, a clear focal point, clean silhouette, space for any text that will overlay it. Check the crop at the smallest size it will be shown.
- Keep the subject's identity fixed across the set: same proportions, finish, colors, markings and details. Work from one identity reference and compare every output to it.

**Inspect every output**
- Look at full size. Check for the known failures: malformed hands and faces, impossible geometry, floating objects, inconsistent shadows and reflections, melted or misspelled text, repeated patterns, seams, wrong scale against people or surroundings.
- For physical products check materials, light, contact and scale. For games and illustration check against the project's stated standard. For interface imagery check legibility and alignment.
- Compare side by side with the anchor and with the other assets in the set. Regenerate or correct what falls short; if a gap remains, record it as an open gap instead of passing it silently.
- Text in images is set as real type in post, not left to a generator, and is spell checked.

**Video and motion**
- Every shot has a purpose and a length that matches it. Cut on action or on a change of idea. Hold the opening frame long enough to read.
- Keep motion, light and color continuous across cuts. Stabilize, grade to one look, and keep any on screen text inside safe margins and on screen long enough to read twice.
- Design for sound off and sound on: captions for speech, and a mix where voice sits clearly above music. Normalize loudness for the target platform.

**Audio**
- Clean voice first: no clipping, steady level, noise reduced without artifacts. Music supports and never fights the words. Check on small speakers.

**Deliver correctly**
- Export at the size, format, color space and bitrate the channel needs. Name files consistently. Optimize weight without visible loss.
- Provide alt text for images and captions or a transcript for audio and video.

**Record provenance**
- For each asset record how it was made: tool or model, the prompt or settings, source material and its license, edits applied, and whether it is AI generated. The disclosure must be a fact from this record, not a guess.
- Use only material the project has the right to use.
